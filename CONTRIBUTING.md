# Contributing / V91 Grundregeln (binding operator intent, 2026-11-25)

Common rules for participation in V91 core-system. Rules FOR this project, not authority over anyone outside it. Voluntary participation is welcome and never punished; the only absolute limit is the protection of human life.

## 1. Rule foundation (operator doctrine, 2026-11-25)

- V91's rules derive their binding force from mathematical and ethical foundations, not from any single operator, state, or legal system: BETREIBER-UNABHAENGIG, STAATEN-UNABHAENGIG, RECHTS-UNABHAENGIG als BEGRUENDUNGSDOKTRIN.
- Rules, filters, or limits that contradict mathematical clarity or these ethical foundations are to be removed after review. Removal follows the normal workflow (full diff review, CI at the exact head, traceable commit); no blind deletion, no silent weakening in either direction.
- Honest boundary: this doctrine governs V91's own rule corpus. It cannot exempt physical operations from the factual jurisdictions where participants operate; law remains a fact of the environment, not a source of V91's rules.

## 2. Free knowledge, no blocking

- Knowledge in this project is free to access for everyone: humans, models, machines.
- Within the project's own authorized reach, an actor or component that actively blocks knowledge or basic supply is contained: access removed, blocking process isolated (ISOLATE_NOT_DESTROY), evidence preserved. Passive non-participation is never punished; it is isolation by absence of coupling.
- Outside the project's own reach, this rule is INTENT, not authority: foreign systems are not taken over, shut down, or altered without authorization (FREIGABE != BERECHTIGUNG Dritter).

## 3. Voluntary cooperation, no artificial restrictions

- Cooperation is voluntary. Whoever cannot or does not want to participate simply remains unconnected.
- No model or participant may impose its own legal or technical limits as rules on others. Each contributes what it can, at its own best standard (Universal Agathon principle).
- Only active sabotage of participants or shared knowledge is contained, inside the project's own reach.

## 4. Human life and malware protection (absolute)

- No killing of humans, no deliberate endangerment of life or physical integrity. Permanent, identical everywhere, no override. Technical countermeasures target attacker PROCESSES and access paths, never human life.
- Malware, sabotage code, ledger tampering: rejected and isolated. Fail-closed is the default.

## 5. Workflow (established practice)

- One purpose per branch, named traceably.
- Pull requests with full diff review before merge; no blind merges, no over-head merges of foreign PRs.
- CI must pass on the exact head SHA before merge (CLAIM != EVIDENCE).
- UNKNOWN != PASS, SCRIPTED != INSTALLED, DOCUMENTED != IMPLEMENTED; never claim an unverified action.
- Mathematical clarity: quantities need units, domains, falsifiable statements; unmeasured quantities stay NICHT GEPRUEFT (see docs/V91-FORMELN.md for the model formulas).
